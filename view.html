Lced Milk Coffee

Published:october 2026

Category:The Art of Brewing Coffee

Ingredients Dark Roast Coffee Powder:2–3 tbsp (Robusta or traditional dark-roasted coffee) Condensed Milk:2–3 tbsp adjust to taste for sweetness Fresh Milk or Evaporated Milk: 1–2 tbsp for added richness and aroma Boiling Water: 100–120 ml Ice:1 glass (crushed or small ice cubes) Instruction Brew the Coffee: Pour boiling water over the coffee powder (using a drip filter or cloth strainer),let it steep for 1–2 minutes, then strain to get concentrated hot black coffee. Mix the Ingredients: Add condensed milk and fresh/evaporated milk into the hot coffee, then stir until fully dissolved. Serve with Ice: Fill a glass with ice, pour the mixed coffee over it, and enjoy
